01 · About

Building intelligent systems at the intersection of deep learning and systems engineering.

I design and ship machine learning systems end-to-end: framing the problem, engineering the data, architecting and training the model, and taking it to reliable, scalable production.

I came to machine learning through full-stack engineering. I spent years building the product side of AI, from interfaces for AI-driven solutions to the real-time frontend of an agentic AI platform, before moving into the models themselves with AI/ML work on an enterprise RAG platform. That background shapes how I work. I care about the details that separate demos from deployed AI: rigorous evaluation, calibrated uncertainty, efficient training and inference, privacy by design, and models people can trust and explain.
